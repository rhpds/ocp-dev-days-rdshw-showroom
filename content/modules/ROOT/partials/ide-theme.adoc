NOTE: Dev Spaces may open with a different color theme from the screenshots. The controls and steps are the same. If text or controls are hard to read, open the Command Palette with kbd:[Ctrl+Shift+P] (kbd:[Cmd+Shift+P] on Mac), run **Preferences: Color Theme**, and choose **Light High Contrast** or **Dark High Contrast**, whichever is clearer on your display. You can change the theme again at any time.
